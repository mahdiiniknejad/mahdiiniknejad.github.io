# 21 — Validation Toolkit (copy-paste field kit for the 30-day plan)

**Purpose:** Make file 15 executable *today*, with zero product and zero code. Everything here is concierge/sales collateral. Swap `[VERTICAL]`, `[NAME]`, `[YOURNAME]`, `[$X]` as needed.
**Not legal advice:** the pilot agreement skeleton below must be reviewed by a lawyer before use (see also TCPA/consent note).

---

## 1. Target list builder (40 owners, 2 verticals)
- Sources: Google Maps ("auto repair near [city]", "optometrist", "med spa"), local FB business groups, vertical forums (Practical Machinist, SelfStorageTalk for fallbacks), LinkedIn (owner/title filter), trade-association member directories.
- Capture per row: business, owner name, vertical, contact (email/phone/DM), channel, status, notes.
- **Pick 2 verticals to test in parallel.** Recommended first pair: **independent auto repair** + **optometry or aesthetic/med-spa**. Avoid dental first (HIPAA).

## 2. Cold outreach (pick the channel the owner actually reads)

**Email / DM (short):**
> Subject: found money sitting in your [VERTICAL] system
>
> Hi [NAME] — I help [VERTICAL] owners recover revenue they already earned but lose when a quote gets declined or a customer lapses and nobody follows up.
>
> I'll do a free 10-minute "recovery audit": you send a simple export, I tell you roughly how much is recoverable right now. No cost, no pitch deck. If the number's worth it, I'll go recover it and you pay me only a share of what actually comes back — $0 if nothing does.
>
> Worth a quick look? — [YOURNAME]

**SMS / even shorter:**
> Hi [NAME], I help [VERTICAL] shops win back revenue from declined quotes & lapsed customers — pay only for what I recover. Can I run you a free recovery estimate? — [YOURNAME]

**Voicemail script:**
> "Hi [NAME], [YOURNAME]. I help [VERTICAL] owners recover revenue from declined estimates and lapsed customers — you only pay a cut of what I actually bring back. I'll do a free estimate first. Call/text me at [number]."

## 3. Discovery-call script (8–12 min) — listening for the kill-thresholds (file 18)
1. "Walk me through what happens when a customer declines a quote / doesn't rebook — who follows up, and how often does it actually happen?" *(tests A1, A4)*
2. "Roughly how many declined estimates / lapsed customers a month? What's an average ticket?" *(tests A2)*
3. "What have you tried — recall tools, a VA, an ISA? What did it cost and did it work?" *(WTP proxy)*
4. "If I recovered [$X] next month and you paid me 20% of it, would that be an easy yes?" *(tests A3 WTP)*
5. "Any worry about me messaging your customers? What would make you comfortable?" *(tests A4 trust)*
6. "Can you export declined estimates / lapsed-customer lists from your system?" *(tests A10 data + A6 consent)*
- **Stop-and-note:** if 18+/20 owners shrug at Q1, the pain is not urgent enough → reframe (file 18, A1).

## 4. Free recovery-audit template (what you send back after they share an export)
> **Recovery snapshot — [Business], [date]**
> - Declined/deferred estimates last 6 mo: **[n]**, total value **[$]**
> - Lapsed customers (no visit > [X] mo): **[n]**, est. value if reactivated at [conservative %]: **[$]**
> - No-show slots last 90 days: **[n]**
> - **Conservative recoverable estimate: [$X]/month**
> - Proposed pilot: I work this list for 30 days, personalised + human-approved messages in your voice. You pay **20% of revenue actually recovered.** $0 if nothing comes back.
> *(Method note: estimate uses [assumptions]; actuals measured during the pilot.)*

## 5. Contingency pilot agreement — plain-language skeleton (LAWYER REVIEW REQUIRED)
> **Revenue-Recovery Pilot — [Business] & [YOURNAME], [date]**
> 1. **Scope:** [YOURNAME] will contact the Business's declined-estimate / lapsed / no-show customers using messages **approved by the Business** before sending.
> 2. **Fee:** **20% of Recovered Revenue.** "Recovered Revenue" = invoiced + collected revenue from a customer contacted under this pilot who books within [30] days of contact. **$0 if none.**
> 3. **Approval & voice:** Business approves message templates; sending pauses on request.
> 4. **Consent/compliance:** Business confirms its customer contact lists have a lawful basis to be messaged (see TCPA/consent note); honors opt-outs immediately.
> 5. **Data:** [YOURNAME] uses data only for this pilot, keeps it confidential, deletes on request.
> 6. **Term:** 30 days; either party may stop anytime; fees due on revenue already recovered.
> 7. **No guarantee / not a covered entity** [if non-dental]; **not legal/medical advice.**
> *(Skeleton only — have a lawyer adapt to your jurisdiction before signing.)*

## 6. TCPA / consent note (read before any SMS)
- US SMS to customers generally needs prior consent; texting purchased/cold lists is high-risk. **Use the Business's own customers who have a prior relationship/consent**, include opt-out ("reply STOP"), and prefer email where consent is unclear. Confirm per-vertical before sending. (If dental: HIPAA + BAA — defer.)

## 7. Tracking sheet (columns to log per pilot — this IS the evidence)
`shop | vertical | list_type(declined/lapsed/noshow) | contacts_sent | replies | bookings | $_recovered | hours_spent | effective_$/hr | top_objection`
- At day 30, compute per vertical: **recovery rate**, **$/account**, **$/hour** → feed the decision gate in file 15 and the kill-thresholds in file 18.

## 8. One-paragraph "what good looks like" at day 30
≥8 owner conversations · ≥3 free audits · **≥2 paid contingency pilots** · first recovered-fee revenue > $0 · a measured recovery rate per vertical · a chosen beachhead. If you hit these, flip the gate (file 20) toward BUILD READY and activate the CTO pack (file 19). If not, pivot to **N7 vertical collections** — at most two reframes, then stop.
