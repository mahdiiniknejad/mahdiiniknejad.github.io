# 12 — Financial Feasibility

**Agent:** Financial Feasibility. All figures are **planning estimates** for a solo technical founder, not audited. Conservative bias.

## Cost to build & operate (lead: Recover)

| Item | Estimate | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| MVP build cost | **~$0–$2k** | Concierge first (no code); then LLM API + no-code/glue. Founder builds. |
| Monthly operating cost (early) | **~$100–$500/mo** | LLM/API, messaging (SMS/email), basic infra |
| AI/model cost per account | **~$3–$15/account/mo** | Drafting + classification; cheap relative to value recovered |
| Messaging cost | **~$10–$40/account/mo** | SMS/email volume |
| Customer acquisition cost (CAC) | **~$50–$300** early (founder-led, low paid) | Mostly time; community + outreach |
| Expected price | **$299/mo + 12–15% recovered** (or pure 20% contingency early) | File 11 |
| Gross margin | **~80%+** | Software economics |

## Unit economics (illustrative, per account)
- Recovered revenue/account/mo: **$2k–$6k** (conservative; depends on vertical & backlog).
- Our revenue/account/mo: **$299 + ~$300–$900 success = ~$600–$1,200.**
- COGS/account/mo: **~$15–$55.**
- **Contribution/account/mo: ~$550–$1,150.**
- Payback period: **< 1 month** (founder-led CAC).
- Break-even customers (to cover ~$1k/mo personal infra + tools): **~2–3 paying accounts.** To replace a salary (~$8k/mo): **~10–15 accounts.**

## Revenue scenarios (solo founder)

| Horizon | Worst case | Realistic | Upside |
|---|---|---|---|
| First 3 months | 0–1 paid pilots; learning | **3–5 contingency pilots**, ~$2–6k total recovered-fee revenue | 8 pilots, clear vertical winner |
| First 12 months | Stuck at 2–3 accounts / reframe needed | **15–35 accounts; ~$120–300k ARR-equiv** | 40+ accounts, $400k+, hire 1 |

## Scoring

| Factor | Score (1–5) | Comment |
|---|---|---|
| Speed to first revenue | **5** | Contingency concierge → cash in weeks |
| Cost to test | **5** | ~$0; no product needed to validate |
| Downside risk | **4** | Time, not capital; reframeable across verticals |
| Scalability | **4** | Software margins; per-vertical integration friction caps it slightly |
| Founder time requirement | **3** | Concierge is labor-heavy until AI automates it |

## Decision: **GREEN.**

The financial logic is unusually favorable for a solo founder: **near-zero cost to validate, self-funding contingency revenue, <1-month payback, ~80% margins, break-even at ~2–3 accounts.** The main financial risk is not capital — it's **whether per-vertical recovery rates are high and consistent enough to make contingency math work**, which the concierge test directly measures.
