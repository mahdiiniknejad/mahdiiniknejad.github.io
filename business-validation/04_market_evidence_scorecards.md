## Decisions & reasoning

- **C1 — Diagnosed-revenue recovery → GREEN.** Best speed-to-revenue (contingency = self-funding), strongest WTP, low behavioral barrier. Weakness: per-vertical incumbents (dental: Weave/RevenueWell). Continue.
- **N6 — Job-shop / field-service quoting ("first quote wins") → GREEN.** Unusually sharp + genuinely underserved at the bottom (state-of-art is spreadsheets + "eyeball"; Paperless Parts exists but low SMB penetration). Weakness: estimate accuracy is hard (liability), conservative slow buyers. Continue.
- **N7 — Vertical collections (self-storage / associations / funeral / pest) → GREEN.** #1-ranked pain by frequency; trivially provable ROI (recovered cash); already paying (staff, funding cos., attorneys). Weakness: "collections SaaS" has horizontal incumbents (Chaser, Upflow) — must go *vertical* to win. Continue.
- **C3 — Scope-creep capture → YELLOW.** Strong pain, only natural home for founder's verifiable-record skill, but **behavioral barrier (owner must confront client) is a possible kill-risk.** Reframe required: the product must *do the asking* (auto-draft + send the change order with a defensible record), not just detect. Continue to reframe.
- **C4 — Dental insurance verification → YELLOW (deferred).** Highest WTP but HIPAA + payer-integration + slow sales make it the wrong *first* build for a solo founder. Park as a high-WTP expansion of C1.
- **C2 — Speed-to-lead → RED (killed).** Pain is GREEN but the market is a funded red ocean (Beside $32M; ElevenLabs/Vapi/Retell; GoHighLevel reseller armies). A solo founder cannot win a voice-AI arms race underfunded. **Killed.**
- **C5 — Recruitment back-office → RED (killed).** Crowded with funded AI-recruiting tools; the core bleed (75–90% sourcing no-reply) isn't fixed by automation. **Killed.**

## Survivors → Competitor Intelligence (Stage 5)

**Four GREEN/strong-YELLOW survivors, all facets of one meta-opportunity ("recover revenue already earned/earnable"):**
1. **C1** — Diagnosed-revenue recovery (high-ticket appointment SMBs)
2. **N7** — Vertical collections (recurring-revenue back-offices)
3. **N6** — Job-shop / field-service quoting (first-quote-wins)
4. **C3** — Scope-creep / change-order capture (project B2B) — *carries founder's Solidity-adjacent angle; behavioral kill-risk flagged*

> The PM Agent notes these four are **strategically the same company at different beachheads**: an *AI agent that plugs into an SMB's system-of-record and converts a manual, dropped, revenue-relevant follow-up into an automated, persistent, personalised one.* Stages 5–12 will determine which beachhead to enter first.
