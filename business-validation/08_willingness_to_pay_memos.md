# 08 — Willingness-to-Pay (Proxy-Based)

**Agent:** Willingness-to-Pay. No interviews yet → use proxies. Question: *will they actually pay, how, and how much?*

## C1 — Diagnosed-revenue recovery (LEAD)

**WTP proxies (all observed in research, file 03):**
1. **Paid competitors exist & charge:** Weave/RevenueWell/Solutionreach/Podium (recall/reactivation) — owners already pay $200–500+/mo.
2. **Expensive manual workaround:** ISAs $900–1,500/mo; VAs $4–17/hr; reactivation campaigns.
3. **Outsourced RCM/collections take 5–30% cuts** — proves outcome-based pricing is accepted.
4. **The recovered revenue is large & measured:** dental $1M–1.5M unscheduled/provider; auto declined-estimates daily.

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Who pays? | The owner (also budget owner & decision-maker) |
| Why would they pay? | It returns money they've **already earned**; ROI is self-evident |
| How much? | **Contingency 15–25% of recovered revenue**, or $200–600/mo SaaS + success fee. If we recover $4k/mo, a 20% fee = $800/mo — comparable to an ISA but pay-for-performance |
| Must-have or nice-to-have? | Must-have *framed as found money*; nice-to-have if framed as "another tool" |
| Budgeted? | Yes — reallocate from recall tools / VA / marketing |
| Urgent? | Latent (invisible leak) → urgency must be **created** by showing "$X recoverable right now" |
| First paid offer | "We'll recover your lost revenue this month. You pay us 20% of what we actually recover. $0 if we recover nothing." |

**Verdict: STRONG WTP.** Contingency removes purchase risk entirely — the strongest WTP structure in the study.

## N7 — Vertical collections
- Proxies: collections staff, agencies, funding companies (funeral pay per-case fees), attorneys. **WTP STRONG**; contingency natural. Storage managers' 10–15 hrs/mo = clear labor budget.

## N6 — Job-shop quoting
- Proxies: dedicated estimators' salaries; Paperless Parts subscriptions; lost 7-figure accounts. WTP exists but pricing is **SaaS (value = won jobs)**, harder to make outcome-based (attribution). **WTP MED-STRONG.**

## C3 — Scope-creep capture
- Proxies: agencies lose $1–5k/mo; buy Ignition/Bonsai. WTP plausible **only if** product recovers billable revenue (could be outcome-priced: % of newly-billed change orders). Behavioral barrier may suppress realised WTP. **WTP MED.**

## Cross-cutting WTP conclusion

The decisive WTP unlock across survivors is **outcome/contingency pricing**: "pay us a share of money we recover/win for you." It (a) eliminates buyer risk, (b) proves WTP unambiguously, (c) self-funds, (d) aligns incentives. This is *only* cleanly possible where the recovered value is **measurable and attributable** — strongest for **C1 (recovered bookings)** and **N7 (recovered cash)**, weaker for N6/C3 (attribution harder). 

**This pushes C1 and N7 ahead of N6 and C3 on WTP.** Passed to Psychology & Positioning (Stage 9).
