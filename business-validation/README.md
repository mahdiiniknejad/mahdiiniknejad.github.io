# Business Validation Pipeline — Index

A multi-agent, evidence-based, deliberately pessimistic discovery of the strongest business opportunity for a solo technical founder (AI + Solidity/smart contracts), run **pain-first** across ~30 industries with technology matched *last*.

## TL;DR
- **Strongest opportunity (pain-first):** **"Recover"** — an AI agent that recovers already-earned-but-lost revenue for high-ticket service SMBs (declined estimates, unscheduled treatment, no-shows, lapsed customers), priced on **contingency**. See [14](14_one_page_business_thesis_for_best_opportunity.md).
- **Honest technology verdict:** This is an **AI** business. **Blockchain/Solidity is not an advantage here and should not be forced** — adjacent crypto/verification/supply-chain markets are crowded or a graveyard. See [03](03_evidence_tables.md) Table F, [13](13_final_ranked_opportunity_report.md).
- **Final gate:** **VALIDATION READY, NOT BUILD READY** — the recoverable-revenue magnitudes are vendor-sourced; prove them with a ~$0, 30-day paid concierge pilot before building. See [20](20_build_readiness_gate.md).
- **#2 backup:** vertical collections (self-storage/associations). **#3:** job-shop/field-service quoting.

## Honesty note
Research sub-agents could not fetch Reddit/G2/Capterra directly (bot-blocked); many quotes are search-snippet-sourced and many dollar magnitudes come from vendors who profit from the pain. **Frequencies and directions are well-corroborated; magnitudes are soft.** Treat this as a rigorous *hypothesis engine* that tells you exactly what to verify with ~20 real customer conversations — not a substitute for them.
