**Agent:** Data Collection (synthesised from ~30-industry parallel web research)
**Strength legend:** STRONG (multiple independent/reputable, or directly observable) · MED (one credible or several weak agreeing) · WEAK (vendor marketing / single forum / inference).
**Global caveat:** Research agents could **not** directly fetch Reddit/G2/Capterra (bot-blocked) — many quotes are search-snippet-sourced, and many dollar magnitudes come from vendors selling the cure (inflation-biased). **Frequencies and directions are well-corroborated; exact magnitudes are soft and must be re-verified in customer interviews.** This is the single most important honesty caveat in the whole pipeline.

## Table F — Technology match (done LAST, per founder mandate)

| Technology | Does it add real value to the lead pain (revenue recovery)? | Evidence |

| **Verifiable/tamper-evident record** | **MAYBE — thin feature only**, and only for the dispute-prone B2B variant (Candidate 3 change-orders): a timestamped, mutually-acknowledged approval log. Even there, a DB + e-signature does ~95%. Not a reason to build on-chain. | Construction CO disputes; Kleros still needs human jurors |

**Conclusion (honest, per founder's rules 5/6/16):** The strongest opportunity is **AI-first**. Blockchain should **not** be forced. The founder's Solidity skill is, for this opportunity, a *near-zero asset* — and saying so is the point of this exercise. The founder's real edge here is **AI + business/research judgement + speed**, not the chain.
