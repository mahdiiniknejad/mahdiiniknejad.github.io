# 11 — Business Model Memos

**Agent:** Business Model. Design & compare models for the lead opportunity (Recover). 

## Model comparison

| Model | Customer | Price | Revenue logic | Gross margin | Sales motion | Fit |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Contingency (success fee)** | Owner | 15–25% of recovered $ | Self-funding; pay-for-performance | High (~80%+ after LLM cost) | Founder-led, frictionless | **Best for validation** |
| **Low SaaS + success fee** | Owner | $199–499/mo + 10–15% recovered | Predictable base + upside | High | Founder-led | **Best at scale** |
| Flat SaaS | Owner | $299–799/mo | Simple, predictable | Very high | Needs proof first | Later |
| Usage-based | Owner | Per message / per recovery | Aligns to volume | High | Harder to explain | Maybe |
| Setup + retainer | Owner | $1–3k setup + monthly | Funds onboarding | High | Friction | For multi-location |
| Marketplace / per-vertical license | SaaS vendors/PMS | Rev-share | Distribution via incumbents | Med | Partnership | Later channel |
| Service + software hybrid | Owner | Done-for-you + tool | Concierge → product | Med (labor early) | Founder-led | **The on-ramp** |

### Recommended model path

1. **Validation (months 0–3): pure contingency, done-for-you.** Frictionless "yes," proves WTP, self-funds, teaches the playbook. Founder does the work (concierge).
2. **Early product (months 3–9): low SaaS base + success fee**, as AI automates the concierge. Base covers infra; success fee captures value & keeps alignment.
3. **Scale (9+): SaaS + success fee, per vertical.** Add setup fee for multi-location.

### Detail (recommended: low SaaS + success fee)
- **Customer:** owner-led high-ticket service SMB.
- **Price:** ~$299/mo + 12–15% of recovered revenue.
- **Revenue logic:** if we recover $4k/mo → $299 + ~$560 = ~$860/mo/account. 30 accounts ≈ **$26k MRR / ~$310k ARR.**
- **Gross margin:** LLM/API + messaging costs are a few $/account/mo → **80%+ gross margin.**
- **First 10 customers:** founder-led, contingency, from one vertical community + warm outreach.
- **First 100:** repeatable per-vertical playbook + case studies + light SaaS self-serve + referrals (happy owners talk).
- **Risks:** (a) recovery rates vary by vertical → contingency math must hold; (b) integration friction per PMS; (c) consent/compliance; (d) incumbents bolt on "AI recovery."
- **Why it fits the customer:** owners hate risk and love found money; pay-for-performance + small base matches how they already buy (recall tools + outcome-based RCM).

## Model notes for fallbacks
- **N7 collections:** identical structure (contingency → SaaS + % recovered). Compliance module justifies higher base.
- **N6 quoting:** **flat SaaS** (outcome attribution too hard for contingency) — $199–499/mo. Weaker WTP structure → another reason A leads.

**Verdict → Financial Feasibility (Stage 12):** Low-SaaS-+-success-fee on a contingency on-ramp. High margin, self-funding, aligned. The model itself is a *competitive advantage* (de-risks the buyer vs. incumbents' flat fees).
