## Worked example (the founder's template)
> **Assumption A3:** Recovery economics make contingency pay.
> **Evidence:** Competitor outcome-pricing exists (RCM 5–30%, ISAs $900–1,500/mo); recoverable-$ vendor-claimed but unverified.
> **Test:** Run 2 paid contingency pilots; log messages, bookings, **$ recovered**, **hours spent**.
> **Kill threshold:** If, across 2 pilots, the effective hourly return is below your acceptable floor (e.g., < $50/hr equivalent) and shows no path to improve via automation → **reframe the beachhead or pivot to N7 vertical collections** (cleaner attribution).

## The three assumptions that can kill the business
**A2 (is revenue actually recoverable?), A3 (does the contingency math pay?), A8 (will incumbents/PMS crush it?).** The first two are **measured for ~$0 in 30 days** (file 15) — which is precisely why this opportunity is worth testing despite weak defensibility: **the cost to learn the truth is near zero, and the truth arrives in weeks.**
