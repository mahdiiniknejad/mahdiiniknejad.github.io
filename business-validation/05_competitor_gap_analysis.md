# 05 — Competitor Intelligence & Gap Analysis

**Agent:** Competitor Intelligence. For the 4 survivors, map who already solves it and find the wedge a solo founder can own *first*.

> Evidence caveat: competitor pricing is mostly "contact sales"; figures are third-party/aggregator-sourced and inflation-prone. Treat as directional.

---

## C1 — Diagnosed-revenue recovery (high-ticket appointment SMBs)

| Competitor type | Examples | What they do | Weakness / gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dental recall/reactivation | Weave, RevenueWell, Solutionreach, NexHealth | Generic reminders, mass recall texts | **Blast, not intelligent recovery**; no persistent personalised follow-up on *declined treatment*; built for dental only |
| Auto-repair follow-up | Bolt On, Shopgenie, Steer | Declined-job tracking, reminders | Low SMB penetration; reminder-grade, not an autonomous recovery agent |
| Horizontal reactivation | Podium, Birdeye | Reviews + messaging + "win-back" campaigns | Generic; not tied to *specific lost revenue events*; owner still configures |
| Manual | Front-desk staff, VAs, ISAs | Work call-back lists | First thing dropped when busy; inconsistent |
| Future entrant | The PMS/POS vendor itself | Could add "AI recovery" | Slow; horizontal; weak at cross-system reconciliation |

**The wedge:** *An autonomous AI agent that ingests a practice's own declined-estimate / unscheduled-treatment / lapsed-patient data and runs persistent, personalised, multi-touch recovery — priced on contingency.* Incumbents do reminders; nobody does **"work the lost-revenue backlog for me, and only charge me for what you recover."** Beachhead: one vertical where ticket is high and incumbents are weakest (auto repair or optometry > dental, where incumbents are strongest).

## N7 — Vertical collections (recurring-revenue back-offices)

| Competitor type | Examples | Gap |
|---|---|---|
| Horizontal AR/collections SaaS | Chaser, Upflow, Gaviti, Paystand | Built for B2B finance teams / generic invoices; **not** tuned to self-storage liens, association dues, pest routes, funeral assignments |
| Vertical software (built-in) | storage: Storable/SiteLink; assoc: Wild Apricot; pest: FieldRoutes | Collections is a weak afterthought module; "buggy," manual |
| Manual / 3rd party | Collections staff, agencies, funding companies (funeral) | Recovery often < labor cost; relationship-damaging |

**The wedge:** *Vertical collections automation for one underserved recurring-revenue niche* (e.g., self-storage delinquency where managers spend 10–15 hrs/mo at only 3–4% delinquency, plus a 90–120-day lien-compliance minefield). Own the niche's specific workflow + compliance, not generic dunning.

## N6 — Job-shop / field-service quoting (first-quote-wins)

| Competitor type | Examples | Gap |
|---|---|---|
| Quoting/estimating SaaS | Paperless Parts, Steel/MIE, Quilty | Mid/large shops; expensive; low penetration at the small-shop bottom |
| Generic | Excel + "eyeball guesstimate" (top-2 methods!) | Slow, inaccurate, owner's nights/weekends |
| Field-service quoting | ServiceTitan/Jobber quote modules | Generic; speed-to-quote still manual |

**The wedge:** *AI that turns an inbound RFQ (email/PDF/drawing) into a fast first-pass quote for small shops* — attacking the "first quote wins / 96% of effort wasted" dynamic at the un-penetrated bottom. Risk: estimate-accuracy liability; conservative buyers.

## C3 — Scope-creep / change-order capture (project B2B)

| Competitor type | Examples | Gap |
|---|---|---|
| Proposal/billing | Ignition, Bonsai, HoneyBook | Handle the *quote/invoice*, not *detect & bill mid-project creep* |
| Early AI point tools | microgaps "AI scope-creep detector" | Nascent, freelancer-grade, detection-only |
| PSA/CLM | Monograph (AEC), Ironclad | Track time/contracts; don't auto-draft change orders from comms |
| Manual | "Absorb it" | The 99%-can't-bill problem |

**The wedge:** *Detect out-of-scope work from project comms/deliverables AND auto-draft the billable change order with a mutually-acknowledged, tamper-evident approval record* — removing both the *detection* gap and the *awkwardness/defensibility* gap. This is the one place the founder's verifiable-record skill is a genuine (if thin) feature.

---

## Cross-cutting: the meta-wedge

All four share the same defensible entry point a solo founder can own before incumbents react:

> **"The autonomous AI follow-up agent for [one vertical]'s single most valuable dropped task — priced on the money it recovers."**

Incumbents are **horizontal** (reminders, generic dunning, generic quoting) or **vertical-but-shallow** (collections as a weak module). The gap is **vertical + autonomous + outcome-priced.** A solo founder wins by going *narrow and deep* in one beachhead where ROI is self-evident and the incumbent is weakest — exactly the opposite of the crowded voice-receptionist red ocean. Passed to Customer Segment (Stage 6).
