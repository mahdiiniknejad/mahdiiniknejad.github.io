## 3. VERIFIED FACTS (directly observable or multiple reputable sources)

- **The AI voice-receptionist / lead-capture space is funded and crowded.** Beside raised a $32M Series A (named funding, Fortune); ElevenLabs, Vapi, Retell, GoHighLevel reseller layer all exist as shipping products. → justifies *killing* idea #17.
- **Prior blockchain trade/supply-chain consortia collapsed** (TradeLens, We.Trade, Marco Polo, Contour) — multiple news sources. → justifies *not forcing blockchain*.
- **FDCPA does not cover B2B commercial debt, but third-party debt collectors face state licensing/registration** (e.g., California DCLA, Florida OFR), and FDCPA applies to consumer-debt collection. → load-bearing reason **#1 beats #2**.
- **TCPA governs U.S. SMS-to-consumer consent.** → a real, manageable constraint on the win-back MVP (use the business's own consented customers + opt-out).
- **A paid reactivation/recall/win-back tool category exists** with real products and pricing (Weave, RevenueWell, Solutionreach, Podium; Bolt On for auto). → owners *already pay* for adjacent fixes (WTP exists).
- **Outcome/percentage pricing is an accepted norm** in these markets (collection agencies and factoring take a cut; RCM takes a percentage; ISAs are paid retainers). → contingency is not an exotic ask.

## 4. STRONG PROXY-SUPPORTED CONCLUSIONS (multiple independent proxies)

- **The meta-pain — earned/earnable revenue lost to manual-process gaps + the unbillable labor of chasing it — is universal**, independently corroborated across ~30 industries (auto, dental, optometry, vet, agencies, law, accounting, trades, landscaping, storage, etc.). *Proxies: trade press, owner forums, vendor categories, job posts, review sites.*
- **Willingness to pay is real**: owners already spend on VAs ($4–17/hr), ISAs ($900–1,500/mo), recall tools ($200–500+/mo), collection agencies, and factoring — revealed preference, not stated.
- **No-shows and declined work are material and frequent**: optometry ~24.8% no-show (ICO); primary care ~19%; multiple verticals 10–20%. The leak is real even if its dollar size is soft.
- **Win-back is regulation- and trust-advantaged vs. collections** (reasoned from verified regulatory facts + consistent forum sentiment that owners fear outsiders chasing their clients). 
- **Late payment is endemic** (Ignition 2025: 97% of agencies; construction payment-delay data) — validates the #2 pain even though #2 loses on friction.

## 5. MEDIUM PROXY-SUPPORTED CONCLUSIONS (one credible proxy, or analogical)

- **Owners will accept AI-assisted, human-approved outreach to their own customers** — inferred from existing adoption of automated recall/reminder tools; direct evidence absent.
- **A meaningful share of declined/lapsed customers can be re-converted with persistent follow-up** — Bolt On reports informing customers raises approval ~50%; manual reactivation yields ~3–5%; directionally positive, magnitude uncertain.
- **Realistic solo year-1 ceiling ~$120–300k** (SOM model, file 10) — a constructed estimate from plausible account counts × fees, not observed.
- **Contingency is the conversion-maximising offer** — inferred from analogous outcome-priced categories; the specific elasticity is untested.

## 6. WEAK PROXY-SUPPORTED CONCLUSIONS (vendor-sourced / single / inferential)

- **Specific recoverable-dollar magnitudes** (e.g., dental "$1M–1.5M unscheduled treatment per provider") — single-vendor blogs, inflation-biased. *Treated as hypothesis, not fact.*
- **Precise market size (TAM/SAM)** — divergent analyst/vendor estimates; used only as labelled-directional.
- **That the concierge automates into a high-margin SaaS without recovery quality degrading** — inferential; closer to an assumption.
- **Which exact beachhead vertical wins** (auto vs. optometry vs. aesthetic) — no decisive proxy; a coin-flip to be settled empirically.

## 7. REMAINING FLAGS

**FLAG 1**
Question unresolved: *What is the real win-back recovery rate per vertical (% of declined/lapsed/no-show customers who rebook when followed up)?*
Reason: No reliable public evidence found; it is intrinsically private operational data that only running the play reveals.
Impact: **High** — it directly determines whether 20% contingency beats the founder's time.
Recommendation: 2 paid contingency pilots (file 22, days 5–7) measuring contacts→bookings→$ recovered→hours.

**FLAG 2**
Question unresolved: *What share of pitched owners actually sign a contingency pilot (real conversion rate)?*
Reason: WTP *structure* is proxy-verified, but conversion % cannot be known without live outreach.
Impact: **Medium** — affects how many conversations are needed to land the first 2 pilots, not whether the pain/WTP exist.
Recommendation: 20-owner outreach test; kill-threshold = <2 serious conversations.

**FLAG 3**
Question unresolved: *Will owners trust AI-assisted outreach to their own customers at acceptable approval effort?*
Reason: Only indirect proxy (recall-tool adoption); no direct evidence.
Impact: **Medium** — mitigated by human-in-the-loop in the MVP.
Recommendation: observe objection rate in discovery calls + first pilot.

**FLAG 4**
Question unresolved: *Will PMS/POS incumbents fast-follow with their own outcome-priced AI recovery?*
Reason: Future competitor behavior; unknowable.
Impact: **Medium** (long-run defensibility, not first-revenue).
Recommendation: treat the 12–24-month head-start as the play; consider PMS partnership as a channel.

*None of these flags change the decision* (see §9).

## 9. WHY #1 STILL WINS DESPITE THE REMAINING FLAGS

1. **The flags are about *magnitude*, not *existence*.** Verified facts + strong proxies establish that the pain is real, universal, and already-paid-for. The unknowns (recovery rate, conversion %, trust) only affect *how good*, not *whether* — and all are cheaply resolved by the 7-day plan.
2. **The cost of being wrong is near zero.** The MVP is manual and contingency-priced: if the recovery economics fail, the founder loses days, not capital. No other survivor offers this asymmetry.
3. **#1 dominates #2 on the only axes that separated them** — regulation (no debt-collector licensing/FDCPA) and trust (friendly win-back vs. chasing a client) — both grounded in *verified facts*, not proxies.
4. **#3 and #4 fail hard rules** (#3 can't be validated without building; #4 has a behavioral barrier), so they can't displace #1 regardless of the flags.
5. **The decision is robust to the flags resolving badly:** if FLAG 1 kills the contingency math, the pre-defined pivot (AR collections, B2B-only) is already specified — so even the downside path is mapped.
