# 09 — Psychology & Positioning

**Agent:** Psychology & Positioning. *Not* manipulation — make the offer clear, trustworthy, relevant. Focus on the lead (C1); principles transfer.

## C1 — Diagnosed-revenue recovery

| Dimension | Finding |
|---|---|
| Core emotional pain | "I work hard and still leave money on the table — and I *know* it" (guilt + frustration about waste) |
| Fear | Looking foolish to peers; the leak being bigger than they think; their customers feeling spammed |
| Frustration | The front desk "never gets to the list"; tried tools that just blast texts |
| Trust barrier | "Will an AI message *my* customers badly and damage *my* reputation?" — the #1 objection |
| Purchase trigger | Seeing a concrete number: "you have ~$X in recoverable revenue sitting in your system right now" |
| Desired feeling after | Relief + control: "the leak is finally being plugged, automatically, and I only pay for results" |
| Words to use | *Recover, found money, already earned, your revenue, you only pay for results, in your voice, you approve everything* |
| Words to avoid | *Blast, campaign, AI bot, automation* (sounds spammy/impersonal), *blockchain/crypto* (irrelevant, erodes trust) |
| Brand tone | Trustworthy operator-to-operator; concrete, numbers-first, low-hype; "we do the awkward follow-up you hate" |
| Visual direction | Clean, calm, money-recovered dashboard; before/after $; no crypto/techy aesthetics |
| Trust signals | Contingency pricing ("$0 if we recover nothing"), human-in-the-loop approval, "messages in your voice," named recovered-$ case studies, opt-out compliance |
| Landing-page promise | **"We recover the revenue your front desk is too busy to chase — and you only pay for what we bring back."** |

**Key behavioral lever:** *loss-recovery framing beats growth framing.* Owners feel the sting of money already-earned-but-lost more than the abstract upside of new leads. Lead with "stop the leak / get back what's yours," not "grow."

## Positioning for the others (brief)
- **N7 collections:** Promise = "Get paid what you're owed — without the awkward calls or lawsuit risk." Trust signal = lien-compliance correctness.
- **N6 quoting:** Promise = "Quote first, win more. Turn any RFQ into a quote in minutes." Trust = human approves every number.
- **C3 scope-creep:** Promise = "Stop giving away work. We catch out-of-scope and bill it — diplomatically." Trust = protects the client relationship; defensible record.

## Cross-cutting positioning principle

All four win on the same frame: **"We do the valuable follow-up you hate doing, you stay in control, and you pay for outcomes."** The emotional engine is **relief from a known, nagging, money-losing chore** + **trust via human-in-the-loop and outcome pricing.** Crucially: **never lead with the technology.** AI is "how," not "what"; blockchain is absent.
