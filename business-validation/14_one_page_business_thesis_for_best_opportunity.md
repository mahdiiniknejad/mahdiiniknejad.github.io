# 14 — One-Page Business Thesis: "Recover"

## The thesis (one paragraph)
High-ticket, owner-led service businesses (auto repair, optometry, aesthetic clinics, dental) silently lose large amounts of **already-earned revenue** — declined estimates, unscheduled treatment, no-show slots, lapsed customers — because the only "fix" is a front-desk human who never gets to the list. **Recover** is an AI agent that connects to the business's system-of-record, surfaces exactly how much revenue is recoverable right now, and autonomously runs persistent, personalised, human-approved follow-up to win it back — **priced on contingency, so the owner pays only for money actually recovered.** AI is the engine; no blockchain is involved, because none is needed.

## Why this wins
- **Universal, validated, already-monetised pain** (file 03): the single most-corroborated wound across the entire service economy is lost/leaked revenue + the cost of chasing it.
- **Strongest WTP structure in the study:** contingency = a near-frictionless "yes" and self-funding revenue.
- **Lowest behavioral barrier:** unlike scope-creep, the recovery action (a friendly follow-up) is normal customer service and **fully automatable** — no confrontation required.
- **Avoids the red ocean:** deliberately *not* the funded voice-receptionist/lead-gen arms race.
- **Founder-fit (honest version):** plays to AI engineering + business judgement + speed. The founder's Solidity skill is **not used here — and that is the correct call.**

## Target first customer
Independent **auto-repair / optometry / aesthetic** business, 1–5 locations, $500k–$5M revenue, owner = buyer = victim, using a PMS/POS that holds the lost-revenue data, already paying for recall tools/ISAs/VAs.

## First paid offer
> "We'll recover the revenue your front desk is too busy to chase. You pay us **20% of what we actually recover** — and **$0 if we recover nothing.** No software to install; we start from a simple export of your declined estimates and lapsed customers."

## MVP recommendation
**Manual concierge, zero code.** Founder takes one shop's export, writes personalised recovery messages (owner-approved), books returns, reports recovered $. Add LLM drafting/classification once 3 pilots prove the recovery rate. Productise only after the contingency math is proven in ≥2 verticals.

## Business & financial logic
- Pricing: contingency 15–25% → low SaaS ($299/mo) + 12–15% success fee at scale.
- ~80% gross margin; ~$0 to validate; **<1-month payback; break-even ~2–3 accounts;** realistic year-1 $120–300k for a solo founder.

## Top risks (and the test that kills/confirms them)
1. **Do recovery rates make contingency pay?** → measured directly by the concierge pilots.
2. **Which beachhead vertical?** → settled by 20 customer conversations.
3. **Will incumbents (Weave/Podium/PMS) copy it?** → win narrow + deep; treat as a head-start, not a moat.
4. **TCPA/consent (and HIPAA if dental)** → opt-out + human approval; avoid dental first.

## What this is NOT
Not a blockchain product. Not a voice receptionist. Not a horizontal "AI for SMBs" platform. Not a product to build before 3 owners have paid for the concierge version.

## Decision
**VALIDATION READY — proceed to the 30-day paid-pilot plan (file 15).** Not "build" yet (file 20).
